Как установить FTB Electron App в Linux

  1. Нажмите кнопку «Скачать · Linux · Flatpak».
  2. Откройте загруженный .flatpakref в центре приложений.
  3. Подтвердите установку из официального Flathub.
Команда для терминалаflatpak install flathub dev.ftb.ftb-app
Безопасная загрузка: кнопка скачивает официальный .flatpakref из подписанного репозитория Flathub. QazDev передаёт оригинальный файл автора и не изменяет установщик.
Оригинальное описание разработчика

Welcome to the FTB App! Our app is designed to provide our users with a simple and easy way to install, update, and customise their FTB Modpacks. We have built-in support for CurseForge modpacks and mods as well as instance management, pack customisation, and a whole lot more. Features: Browse and download FTB and CurseForge modpacks Simple and Easy to Use: Our app is designed to be simple and easy to use for all users, regardless of their experience with modpacks. Instance Management: Change your modpacks name, artwork, Java version, settings, and more. Pack Customisation: Customise your modpacks with ease, add more mods from CurseForge, or upload your own mods. You can also customise your modpacks settings, such as the amount of RAM, Java Version, JVM Arguments, Startup arguments and more. Sharing: Share your minecraft modpacks with friends with just a few clicks.
