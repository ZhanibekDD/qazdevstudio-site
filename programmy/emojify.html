Оригинальное описание разработчика

Emojify is a lightweight emoji picker designed for the modern Linux desktop. Built with C++ and GTK4, it offers a seamless and responsive experience that feels right at home on GNOME. Key Features: ⚡ Fast startup thanks to a pure C++ and GTK4 implementation. 🎨 A clean interface that follows your system's light or dark mode. 🫆 Operates entirely locally without tracking or external network calls.
