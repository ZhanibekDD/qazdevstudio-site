Оригинальное описание разработчика

DOS Browser is a specialized, lightweight browser designed with retro computing enthusiasts in mind. It features hardware acceleration specifically optimized for DOS/Win9x-based games, allowing them to run more efficiently and at much higher speeds compared to standard browsers like Chrome. Whether you’re exploring DOS applications, playing classic games, or navigating retro-themed websites, DOS Browser ensures a smoother and more enjoyable experience. Install DOS Browser from Flathub today and rediscover the golden era of computing with cutting-edge enhancements for your classic favorites!
