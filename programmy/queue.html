Оригинальное описание разработчика

Queue is a simple, fast task manager. Organize your tasks into groups, mark them as done or important, reorder them with drag and drop, and keep your data safe with built-in backups. Features: Organize tasks into custom, color-coded groups. Mark tasks as completed or important. Write rich task descriptions with headings, text styles, colors and lists. See the date when each task was completed. Reorder groups and tasks with drag and drop. Export and restore all your data as JSON. Light and dark styles, available in several languages.
