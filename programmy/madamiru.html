Оригинальное описание разработчика

Madamiru is a cross-platform media player written in Rust that can automatically play and shuffle multiple videos, images, and songs at once in a grid layout. Features: Customizable layout with multiple groups of dynamic media Video formats: AVI, M4V, MKV, MOV, MP4, WebM, plus any others supported by GStreamer Image formats: BMP, GIF, ICO, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, SVG, WebP Audio formats: FLAC, M4A, MP3, WAV Subtitles are supported within MKV (but not as separate files)
