Оригинальное описание разработчика

Amharic Bible is a modern, offline Bible reader built with KDE Kirigami and Qt6. It features a clean and elegant reading experience, full offline access to the Amharic Bible text, and an interface inspired by WordProject. The app supports both light and dark themes, adjustable font size, and quick navigation between books and chapters.
