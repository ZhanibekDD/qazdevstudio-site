Оригинальное описание разработчика

Amiberry lets you experience the Commodore Amiga, a legendary home computer from the late 1980s and 1990s known for its groundbreaking graphics, sound, and gaming library. Emulate a wide range of Amiga models, from the classic A500 to the powerful A4000. Play thousands of games, run productivity software, or explore the demoscene — all with accurate hardware emulation and modern conveniences like save states, shader effects, and gamepad support. Features include a built-in game launcher for easy one-click play, customizable controls, support for multiple disk image formats, and a full-featured settings interface for fine-tuning every aspect of the emulated hardware.
