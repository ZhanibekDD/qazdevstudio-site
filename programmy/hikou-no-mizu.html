Оригинальное описание разработчика

Hikou no mizu is a 2D fighting game with anime-inspired graphics. Fights take place in interactive arenas of various sizes. Both networked multiplayer and local games are possible. Joysticks are supported and a simple AI is included for solo playing. Playable characters fight using natural powers such as water, lightning, or fire. Currently, three characters are playable: Hikou, Takino and Hana.
