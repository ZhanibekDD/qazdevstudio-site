Как установить Crosswords в Linux

  1. Нажмите кнопку «Скачать · Linux · Flatpak».

Оригинальное описание разработчика

A simple and fun game of crosswords. Load your crossword files, or play one of the included games. Features include: Support for shaped and colored crosswords Loading .ipuz, .jpuz, .xd, and .puz files Hint support, such as showing mistakes and suggesting words Dark mode Locally installed crosswords as well as support for 3rd party downloaders
