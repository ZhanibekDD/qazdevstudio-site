Оригинальное описание разработчика

jdSystemMonitor is an advanced, desktop-independent system monitor for Linux. Its goal is to provide as much information about your system as possible, with a focus on process management. Features: View information about your distribution Manage your processes Monitor CPU, RAM, and swap usage View running Flatpaks See all programs in your autostart View all mounted filesystems See your installed packages View all users on your system Check your system configuration
