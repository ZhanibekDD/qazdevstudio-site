Оригинальное описание разработчика

Bookup is a user-friendly note-taking application that streamlines the process with the use of markdown syntax. Whether you're a student, professional, or simply someone who loves to jot down ideas, Bookup offers a seamless and efficient way to capture and organize your notes. Bookup draws inspiration from the highly acclaimed Notes-up application. We have taken the best aspects of Notes-up and enhanced them to create an even more user-friendly note-taking experience. Bookup is built in C for optimal speed and efficiency, offering quick performance and a low memory footprint.
