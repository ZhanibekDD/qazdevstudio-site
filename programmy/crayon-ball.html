Оригинальное описание разработчика

Color matching, ball popping, and physics fun! When a group of four same-colored balls touch, they will burst and an avalanche of new balls will tumble in to take their place. Quickly and strategically click to burst a few more to set up the next match even before they settle into place! Originally released in 2007 for the Mac as ScribBall, and re-masted as Crayon Ball in 2009. After finding the source code we decided to open source it. Enjoy!
